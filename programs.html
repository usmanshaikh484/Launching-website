Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Beast Boogie's programs and events.

What's included in a birthday party booking? +

Each birthday party includes a dynamic performance showcasing advanced breaking moves, interactive teaching sessions where guests learn basic moves, and a full dance party. The typical package runs 60–90 minutes depending on your group size. Beast Boogie brings the energy, music setup, and enthusiasm to make your party unforgettable.

Do I need breaking experience to participate? +

No experience necessary! Beast Boogie's programs are designed for all levels — from complete beginners to advanced breakers. Every session includes beginner-friendly moves, and participants progress at their own pace. Whether it's your first time or you've been breaking for years, there's something for you.

What's the difference between camps and workshops? +

Camps are multi-day or multi-week programs (typically 1–4 weeks) covering foundational breaking, building community, and sustained skill development. Workshops are shorter, focused sessions (2–6 hours) targeting specific techniques like footwork, freezes, or power moves. Choose a camp for comprehensive training or a workshop to drill down on a specific skill.

How do I book a program or event? +

Visit the Book Now page, fill out our event request form with your details (date, group size, program type), and we'll get back to you within 24 hours with availability and pricing. For custom events or large groups (50+ people), email us directly at info@beastboogie.com for a personalized quote.

Can you accommodate large corporate events? +

Absolutely. Beast Boogie has entertained corporate audiences of 100+ at team-building events, conferences, and company celebrations. We customize performances and interactive experiences to fit your vibe and goals. Contact us to discuss your event requirements.

What's the best age for breaking lessons? +

Kids as young as 4 can start learning breaking basics. That said, 6–8 is an ideal age when kids have better body awareness and coordination. Teens and adults can start anytime — it's never too late. Beast Boogie has taught students from age 4 to 60+, and everyone discovers something new.

Do you offer virtual or online programs? +

Yes! The B-Boy Dojo is Beast Boogie's online academy with comprehensive video courses covering footwork, top rock, freezes, and choreography. Perfect for learning at your own pace. Check out the Learn Online section to explore courses and sign up.
