Bring TikTok Energy to Your Gymnasium

Beast Boogie has taught 100,000+ students in person through school assemblies that leave audiences electrified. His performances combine jaw-dropping breakdancing with authentic lessons about confidence, perseverance, and self-expression.

With 28 years of professional breaking experience and millions of social media followers, Beast Boogie brings the energy your students actually care about — and keeps them engaged from the opening beat to the standing ovation.
