About Anthony

Meet Anthony "Beast Boogie" Silva

With 28 years of breaking experience, Anthony Silva is one of the most respected B-Boys in the world. Based in Houston, Texas, he's spent nearly three decades perfecting his craft — from the streets where it all began to the world's biggest stages.

As a performer, Anthony has graced stages at the NBA All-Star Game, Art Basel, the Houston Rockets, major music festivals, and the legendary Red Bull BC One. His competitive record speaks for itself: over 80 battle wins across major competitions worldwide.

But performance is just half the story. Anthony's true passion is education and inspiration. Whether through school assemblies, his online B-Boy Dojo academy, or community workshops, he's dedicated to sharing the discipline, confidence, and self-expression that breaking has given him — reaching millions of people globally.

Dance isn't just what Anthony does. It's his life's mission to help others discover their own power through movement.
